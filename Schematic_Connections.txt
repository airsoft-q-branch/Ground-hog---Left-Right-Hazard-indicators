========================================
GROUND HOG LED INDICATOR CIRCUIT
Schematic & Wiring Connections
========================================

POWER DISTRIBUTION
==================

    12V Battery (+)
         |
         +-- [30A Main Fuse]
         |
         +-- [Relay VCC +12V]
         |
         +-- [LED +12V Supply]


    Battery (-) / GND
         |
         +-- [Common Ground Point]
         |
         +-- [Relay GND]
         |
         +-- [Arduino GND]
         |
         +-- [LED GND]


BUCK CONVERTER (12V to 5V)
===========================

    12V Battery (+) ----+---- [30A Fuse] ---- [Buck Converter IN+]
    Battery GND ----+---- [Buck Converter IN-]
    
    [Buck Converter OUT+] ---- [Arduino 5V]
    [Buck Converter OUT-] ---- [Arduino GND]


ARDUINO MICROCONTROLLER (Uno/Nano)
===================================

Pin Configuration:

    Arduino 5V  ---- Buck Converter Output (+5V)
    Arduino GND ---- Common Ground (Battery -)
    
    Arduino D2  ---- [SW1 - 10kΩ Pull-down to GND] ---- [Switch to 5V]
    Arduino D3  ---- [SW2 - 10kΩ Pull-down to GND] ---- [Switch to 5V]
    Arduino D4  ---- [Relay Module IN1 - Left Indicator]
    Arduino D5  ---- [Relay Module IN2 - (Optional redundant Left)]
    Arduino D6  ---- [Relay Module IN3 - Right Indicator]
    Arduino D7  ---- [Relay Module IN4 - (Optional redundant Right)]
    Arduino GND ---- [Relay Module GND]


SWITCH CONNECTIONS
==================

SW1 (Left Indicator):
                     
    5V ---- [Momentary Switch] ---- [0.1µF Cap to GND] ---- [10kΩ Resistor to GND]
                                              |
                                        [to Arduino D2]

SW2 (Right Indicator):

    5V ---- [Momentary Switch] ---- [0.1µF Cap to GND] ---- [10kΩ Resistor to GND]
                                              |
                                        [to Arduino D3]


RELAY MODULE (4-Channel 12V)
=============================

    Relay VCC    ---- 12V Battery (+)
    Relay GND    ---- Battery (-) [Common Ground]
    
    IN1 (D4)     ---- Arduino D4  --> [1N4007 Flyback Diode] --> CH1 Coil
    IN2 (D5)     ---- Arduino D5  --> [1N4007 Flyback Diode] --> CH2 Coil
    IN3 (D6)     ---- Arduino D6  --> [1N4007 Flyback Diode] --> CH3 Coil
    IN4 (D7)     ---- Arduino D7  --> [1N4007 Flyback Diode] --> CH4 Coil
    
    NOTE: 1N4007 Diodes connected with cathode (+) to relay coil positive,
          anode (-) to relay coil negative (across coil terminals)


LED INDICATOR CONNECTIONS (12V)
===============================

Configuration: Pair front + rear LEDs on same relay channel

SIMPLIFIED 2-CHANNEL OPTION (Recommended):

Channel 1 (Left - Relay CH1):
    12V (+) ---- [10A Fuse] ---- Relay CH1 NO (Normally Open) Terminal
                                          |
                                          +---- [Front Left LED +12V]
                                          |
                                          +---- [Rear Left LED +12V]
    
    Relay CH1 COM (Common) ---- [All LED GND] ---- Battery (-)

Channel 2 (Right - Relay CH2):
    12V (+) ---- [10A Fuse] ---- Relay CH2 NO (Normally Open) Terminal
                                          |
                                          +---- [Front Right LED +12V]
                                          |
                                          +---- [Rear Right LED +12V]
    
    Relay CH2 COM (Common) ---- [All LED GND] ---- Battery (-)


FULL 4-CHANNEL OPTION (Redundancy):

Channel 1 (Front Left - Relay CH1):
    12V (+) ---- [10A Fuse] ---- Relay CH1 NO ---- [Front Left LED +12V]
    Relay CH1 COM ---- [Front Left LED GND] ---- Battery (-)

Channel 2 (Rear Left - Relay CH2):
    12V (+) ---- [10A Fuse] ---- Relay CH2 NO ---- [Rear Left LED +12V]
    Relay CH2 COM ---- [Rear Left LED GND] ---- Battery (-)

Channel 3 (Front Right - Relay CH3):
    12V (+) ---- [10A Fuse] ---- Relay CH3 NO ---- [Front Right LED +12V]
    Relay CH3 COM ---- [Front Right LED GND] ---- Battery (-)

Channel 4 (Rear Right - Relay CH4):
    12V (+) ---- [10A Fuse] ---- Relay CH4 NO ---- [Rear Right LED +12V]
    Relay CH4 COM ---- [Rear Right LED GND] ---- Battery (-)


COMPLETE BILL OF MATERIALS (BOM)
================================

Microcontroller & Logic:
  - Arduino Uno or Arduino Nano (1x)
  - 12V to 5V Buck Converter (1x)

Relays & Switching:
  - 4-Channel 12V Relay Module (1x) [use 2+ channels]
  - 1N4007 Diodes (4x) [flyback protection]

Passive Components:
  - 10kΩ Resistor (2x) [switch pull-down]
  - 0.1µF Ceramic Capacitor (2x) [switch debounce]

Power Protection:
  - 30A Automotive Fuse (1x) [main line]
  - 10A Automotive Fuses (2-4x) [per LED pair]
  - Fuse Holders (3-5x)

Switches:
  - Momentary Push-Button Switches (2x) [SW1, SW2]

Wiring:
  - 12 AWG Automotive Wire (red, black) - Main power lines
  - 16 AWG Wire (colored) - Signal/control lines
  - Butt Connectors & Spade Terminals
  - Heat Shrink Tubing (assorted sizes)

LEDs:
  - 12V LED Indicators (4x) [Front Left, Rear Left, Front Right, Rear Right]
    Recommended: 1.5-2A rated automotive LEDs

Optional:
  - Status LED + 220Ω Resistor [system indicator]
  - Power Distribution Block [terminal management]

SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS
====================

1. Always fuse positive 12V lines (30A main, 10A per branch)
2. Ensure all relay coils have flyback diodes (1N4007)
3. Keep switch wiring away from high-current paths
4. Use appropriate gauge wire for current draw
5. Secure all connections to prevent vibration loosening
6. Place relay module near battery for short heavy-gauge runs
7. Test system with LEDs unpowered first
8. Verify relay operation before operating vehicle
